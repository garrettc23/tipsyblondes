# Tipsy Blondes design system

- Use direct headings. Never use eyebrows, overlines, or decorative section labels above a heading.
- Never put draft caveats, production notes, or placeholder explanations in the website UI. Keep unresolved content and asset requests in the internal handoff; omit unconfirmed material from the site.
- Use upright serif headings and readable sans-serif body text. Do not use italic typography.
- Present “TIPSY BLONDES” prominently, with “MOBILE BAR AND BARTENDING” as the service descriptor. Both lines use all caps, without OC. Keep the desktop brand centered above navigation and the mobile header compact.
- Use a light, feminine palette: ivory (#fffdf9), cream (#fbf6f1), faint blush (#f7ece8), warm brown ink (#443b37), and muted rose-brass accents (#865d50). Keep photography bright and large, with subtly rounded 12px corners. Use 16px corners for cards and inset form panels. Full-width hero and closing photo banners remain edge to edge. Keep most page sections light; use a full-width cocktail-cups photo with a dark overlay and centered copy for the final inquiry.
- Make the all-caps brand prominent (64–72px on desktop) and navigation readable (16px desktop). Set the service descriptor beneath the wordmark to 12px on phones and 14px on tablet/desktop, retaining its light weight and letter spacing. The opening hero is full width with large ivory text vertically centered over its photographic background. Keep the photograph edge to edge with no faded side blocks. The hero uses the guests-with-cocktails photograph, with a 55% vertical focal point on wider screens. Keep the hero compact: 500–600px on phones and 560–800px on larger screens, responsive to viewport height. Do not expand the hero in proportion to viewport width. Anchor the large copy in the opening view, retain an even translucent contrast overlay with no directional fade, and never stretch or fabricate the bar. Use a crop that keeps the cocktail toast recognizable; the hero no longer needs to show the physical bar.
- Keep the homepage on a continuous cream background, with a full-width blush testimonial band. Separate sections with generous spacing instead of horizontal rules. Present benefits as a clean numbered list without horizontal rules. Put each homepage testimonial in a soft ivory tile with rounded corners over the blush band. Review tiles have no drop shadows; the Reviews page uses blush tiles. FAQs use very faint blush tiles (#f7ece8) with strong espresso headings and dark brown answers, keeping clear separation from the cream page without outlines. Use the full-width cocktail-cups closing inquiry banner, followed by the light footer.
- Alternate photo-and-copy sections on desktop: image left, right, left; the closing inquiry uses full-width photography. Keep body copy left-aligned within its column and use a natural single-column reading order on mobile.
- Protect text contrast over photography at every breakpoint. Small text requires at least 4.5:1 and large text at least 3:1.
- Lead with the people: introduce Amber and Taylor in the hero copy and place their photograph and introduction directly below it, before service details.
- Write in Amber and Taylor’s first-person voice, grounded in their notes and confirmed background. Explain the work behind the drinks and what it means for the host. Use specific details, natural sentences, and genuine invitations; avoid generic luxury claims, invented personal anecdotes, and repetitive slogan-like copy. Keep customer testimonials verbatim. Shared narrative copy lives in `lib/content.ts`.
- For the local review, show three visually distinct setups from existing event photos with descriptive labels. Only the white panel bar is confirmed as an offering. The natural wood bar and white bar with arched shelving require client confirmation of ownership, availability, and final names before publication. Keep provisional setups out of metadata and structured data; do not imply they are included in a package.
- Use clear button-style calls to action in page content: filled brown for every standalone CTA throughout the site, outlined only for secondary actions grouped alongside a primary button. Retain filled light primary buttons over the hero photo and dark closing panel. Use 16px labels, and at least 56px height. Use concise action wording. Never append decorative arrows to buttons or links, including external links and footer actions. Keep navigation and footer links as ordinary links.
- Use custom-quote inquiries. “Chat with us” opens /contact at the top of the page. In navigation it uses the same typography and styling as the other links, without an arrow.
- Preserve useful functional labels, accessible names, service terms, and form fallback links.
- Keep all changes local until publication is explicitly authorized.

- Booking step descriptions use 16px text and semibold headings. Booking steps and contact-option tiles use faint blush fills (#f7ece8) with strong espresso (#281e1a) text for contrast; contact tiles are rounded and borderless. Menu and Gallery introductions have 32px bottom padding followed by 16px content top padding. Service inclusion tiles use wider columns to keep labels on one line where space permits.

- Use one solid color throughout each heading. Do not highlight individual words in a second color. Prefer specific, conversational headings over paired slogans. FAQs name Tipsy Blondes where appropriate and answer the question in their first sentence, then explain.

- Keep `/pricing` as Bar & Packages. Present the original Pop Up Bar and Tipsy Blonde packages without prices, with the Tipsy Blonde package first. Show both packages fully expanded in equal-width vertical cards side by side from tablet widths, stacking on phones. Keep the bar-included check/X in each inclusion list and align the inquiry buttons. Put the alcohol-supply paragraph centered below both cards. Show the three photographed bars in the following section rather than repeating the white-bar photograph beside the packages. Only publish add-ons confirmed by the client.

- Use the single faint blush token (#f7ece8) everywhere pink appears, including testimonials, FAQs, booking steps, contact tiles, and package callouts. Footer contact links use the same plain-link styling as other footer links, without underlines.

- Browser tab icons use a compact TB monogram with a shared serif stem and a clear vertical gap between the T’s top stroke and the B’s upper bowl, ivory lettering (#fffdf9), warm brown background (#443b37), and rounded corners. The editable vector is app/icon.svg; app/icon.png and the 16/32/48px app/favicon.ico are exports. Keep the full wordmark out of tiny tab icons.

- Keep page-intro supporting copy broad: up to 56rem for full-width page headings, or the full text-column width in split layouts. Avoid narrow paragraph caps beneath wide headings. Package alcohol information is a quiet small-text note, not a headline or colored warning panel.

- Use official brand artwork for featured clients, preserving proportions. Render all four in black with a matching light-gray (#aaa6a3) hover and keyboard-focus color applied only to the artwork. Remove source backgrounds in the SVG rendering filter; do not fade the entire link or use blend modes that expose image rectangles. Set optical sizes and view boxes per logo to balance visible artwork rather than matching file dimensions. DermFx, Dose, LSPACE, and Swirl Boutique logos are sourced from their websites. The user confirmed dosedaily.co as the intended Dose brand. Exclude West Elm until the event and permission are confirmed. Curated social thumbnails must link to their matching Instagram posts, with explicit accessible labels and new-tab behavior.

- Show the full wordmark and descriptor at the page top. As the page scrolls, let the brand move offscreen and pin only the navigation tabs in a 68px strip on desktop; phones retain a compact 64px menu strip. Restore the brand at the top without shifting page content. Use the compact height for anchor offsets and the current visible header height to constrain the mobile dropdown.

- Center the mobile brand on the viewport independently of the menu toggle. Keep the toggle clear of the wordmark at every width. Menus use gentle fade/slide motion and respect reduced-motion settings; navigation has no hover fill. Underline hovered links in dark espresso; mark the current page with a persistent rose-brass underline and matching text, including while hovered. Retain visible keyboard focus outlines.

- Testimonial quotes use upright Lora serif in medium (500) weight, dark espresso, at 22px on phones and 24px on larger screens with 1.55 line height. Its rounded forms and fuller strokes preserve the soft editorial style while improving long-quote readability. Keep Cormorant Garamond for headings and decorative quotation marks. Reviewer names use 16px regular-weight muted brown (#70615a) text with natural capitalization, keeping emphasis on the dark espresso quote.

- Internal page-navigation buttons, navigation tabs, and footer links use PageLink: start at scroll position zero with the full wordmark visible, including same-page activation. Do not point page-navigation CTAs at section anchors. Keep in-page accessibility links and external links separate.

- Use Lora (font-accent) for FAQ questions, the Why book Tipsy Blondes benefit titles and matching numbers, and booking-step headings. Keep Cormorant for the brand and large section headings, and Jost for body copy and controls.

- Shared-link previews use `public/media/tipsy-blondes-social.png` at 1200 × 630, with the faint blush background and warm brown lettering. Center the Cormorant wordmark at 136px and the uppercase Jost descriptor at 52px so both remain readable in small previews.
